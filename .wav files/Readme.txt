Some samples of recorded audio data collected from the IIT Guwahati campus are provided here for learning and educational purposes. The `.wav` files follow the naming format `YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS`. For example, `20251112_203537.wav` indicates that the recording was made on 12 November 2025 at 20:35:37, where `2025` represents the year, `11` the month, `12` the day, and `20:35:37` the recording time in HH:MM:SS format.
